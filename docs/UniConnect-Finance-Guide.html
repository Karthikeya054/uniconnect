Finance Guide · for new users

Spending money, the UniConnect way.

Two tools handle every rupee: Budget Proposals to plan and get sign-off before an event, and Petty Cash to get a small advance now and settle it with the bill. This guide walks both, end to end.

Budget

Planning an event or activity? Use a Budget Proposal — list what you'll spend, get it approved, then run the event and report back with bills.

Petty Cash

Need a small amount of cash right now (materials, refreshments, a quick purchase)? Raise a Petty Cash request, get it approved, receive the money, and submit the bill within 7 days.

Guide 01

Budget Proposals

Get money approved before you spend it — for a fest, a workshop, a celebration, any planned activity.

  1. Draft & submit the proposal

    Open Budgeting → New Proposal. Add a title, the line items you'll spend on, the estimated total, the proposed date, and attach any quotes or supporting files. Hit Submit.

    You — Proposer (PM / BOA)→Submitted

  2. Review

    Reviewers open your proposal and check it. They can approve it, Request Changes (it comes back to you to edit and resubmit), or Reject it.

    CMA Manager · Reviewers→Under ReviewChanges Requested

  3. CMA approval (Level 1)

    The CMA Manager (Pravalika) gives the first approval. What happens next depends on the amount — see the ₹10,000 rule below.

    CMA Manager — Pravalika→CMA Approved

  4. Final approval

    Once fully approved you'll be notified that you can go ahead and run the event. Procurement / spending can begin.

    Admin — Karthik (if ≥ ₹10,000)→Approved

  5. After the event — report & bills

    When the event is done, upload your post-event report and the bills. Reviewers check the outcome and close the proposal.

    You — Proposer→Report SubmittedClosed

Key rule The ₹10,000 line

Where a proposal ends depends on its estimated total:

Under ₹10,000

CMA approval (Pravalika) is final. Procurement can proceed straight away — no admin sign-off needed.

₹10,000 and above

Needs Admin approval (Karthik) on top of CMA approval before the proposal is fully approved.

Submitted Under Review Changes Requested CMA Approved Approved Report Submitted Closed Rejected
Guide 02

Petty Cash

A small cash advance for an immediate need — approved in two steps, then settled with the bill.

  1. Raise the request

    Open Petty Cash → New Request. Enter the purpose, amount, category, when you need it by, and the linked activity. Submit it.

    You — Requester→Awaiting L1

  2. Level 1 approval

    Pravalika reviews the request and the event it's for. She approves it (or sends it back for changes / rejects it). Approving passes it up to Level 2.

    Level 1 — Pravalika→Awaiting L2

  3. Level 2 approval (budget check)

    Satish checks the budget and gives the final approval, confirming the approved amount. Only after this can money move.

    Level 2 — Satish→Approved

  4. Disburse the money

    Finance pays you and records it: the amount, the mode (UPI / NEFT / Cash), the UTR / reference, and an uploaded proof — the payment screenshot or the confirmation mail. This starts a 7-day bill deadline.

    Finance — Sasi→Disbursed

  5. Submit your bill

    Attach the bill(s) for what you actually spent — bill number, date, vendor, amount, and a photo or PDF. Do this within 7 days of getting the money.

    You — Requester→Bill Submitted

  6. Verify the bill

    Finance checks the bill against the advance and marks it verified (or rejects an invalid one).

    Finance→Bill Verified

  7. Settle & close

    Finance enters the actual amount spent. The system works out whether you return leftover cash or are owed a top-up, and closes it out.

    Finance→Settled

Two approvals, always in order

Petty cash needs both sign-offs: Pravalika (Level 1) first, then Satish (Level 2) for the budget check. The request only becomes payable after Level 2 — no one can skip a level, and the money is disbursed only once it reaches Approved.

Questions

Good to know

Budget Proposal or Petty Cash — which one?

If you're planning an event and want the spend approved ahead of time, raise a Budget Proposal. If you need a small amount of cash in hand now and will bring back a bill, use Petty Cash. A large planned event goes through a budget proposal; a quick ₹1,000 purchase goes through petty cash.

Who approves my petty cash, and in what order?

Always two people, in order: Pravalika approves first (Level 1), then Satish gives the final budget approval (Level 2). Only after Satish approves does finance disburse the money. You'll get a mail at each step.

Satish said he'd pay me and send a confirmation mail — where does that go?

At the Disburse step. Whoever pays opens the request, enters the amount and the UTR / reference, and uploads the confirmation mail or screenshot as the payment proof. That's the evidence attached to the record.

What if I spend more or less than the advance?

The Settle step handles it. Finance enters what you actually spent; if you spent less, you return the leftover; if you spent more, you're owed a top-up. Either way the request is then closed.

How long do I have to submit the bill?

7 days from the day the money is disbursed. The request shows the due date, and it's flagged as overdue if the bill is late.

Why does the ₹10,000 line matter for budgets?

Under ₹10,000, CMA approval (Pravalika) is the final sign-off and you can proceed. ₹10,000 and above also needs Admin approval (Karthik) before the proposal is fully approved.

I'm on the Facilities / finance team — why do I only see one campus?

You shouldn't any more. Facilities and finance now see every university in both Budgeting and Petty Cash. If a page still looks scoped to one campus, do a hard refresh (Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + R) or sign out and back in.
